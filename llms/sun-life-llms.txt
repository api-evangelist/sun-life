# Sun Life

> Sun Life Financial Inc. is a Canadian multinational life and health insurer and asset manager headquartered in Toronto, with operations in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, Ireland and Asia. Sun Life publishes no public developer portal of its own; the only public, machine-readable APIs in the Sun Life group are the FHIR R4 interoperability APIs of DentaQuest, its U.S. dental subsidiary, released under the CMS Interoperability and Patient Access Final Rule (CMS-9115-F).

Generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline on 2026-07-25. method: generated. No provider-published llms.txt exists (https://www.sunlife.com/llms.txt returns 404).

## APIs

- [DentaQuest FHIR Patient Access API](https://developers.dentaquest.com/apis): FHIR R4 read access to a member's Patient, Coverage, Organization, Practitioner and RelatedPerson records. SMART on FHIR authorization. Base URL https://api.dentaquest.com/FhirPatientAccess/v1
- [DentaQuest FHIR Provider Directory API](https://developers.dentaquest.com/apis): FHIR R4 Da Vinci PDex Plan-Net directory — Practitioner, PractitionerRole, Organization, Location, HealthcareService, InsurancePlan, Endpoint. Azure APIM subscription key. Base URL https://api.dentaquest.com/FhirProviderDirectory
- [DentaQuest FHIR Metadata API](https://developers.dentaquest.com/apis): Anonymous FHIR CapabilityStatement endpoint. Base URL https://api.dentaquest.com/fhirmetadata
